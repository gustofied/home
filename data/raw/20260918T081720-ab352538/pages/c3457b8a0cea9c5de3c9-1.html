Los Angeles Data Centers

Los Angeles Vernon Data Center

(LAX2) 3094 E. Vernon Avenue - Vernon, CA 90058

Located in Vernon, California — just minutes from downtown Los Angeles and major transportation routes — our LAX2 data center is built to support the performance, scale, and connectivity demands of modern enterprise workloads. As DataBank’s second Los Angeles-area facility, LAX2 delivers over 140,000 square feet of purpose-built data center space and 32MW of critical IT power. With robust interconnection capabilities and access to multiple on-site carriers, this facility serves as a strategic hub for organizations requiring low-latency access across Southern California and beyond.

Power

  • 32MW Critical IT Load
  • 240V, 415V, 3P AC Power
  • 100% Renewable Energy Source
