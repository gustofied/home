      Definitions and method

      What can we trust?

      We use the figures on each facility page and compare them with market cards and totals. Campus totals stay out of facility rows. Missing values remain unknown.

      Operating status, current availability, GPU configurations, pricing, contract terms and measured latency are outside this dataset.

      DFW12's source page describes the facility in the future tense. Its advertised specification alone does not establish commissioned or available capacity.

      From page to finding

      1. Save HTML with source URLs, retrieval times and hashes.
      2. Parse labelled specifications, normalize units and validate facilities.
      3. Record missing data, rejected rows and conflicting claims.
      4. Calculate findings and publish only after quality checks pass.

      Retrieval time is not the date a specification was last updated. Results can be rebuilt from the saved HTML without network access.
